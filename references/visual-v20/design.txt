LOCKED ART DIRECTION  /  ENFORCE GLOBALLY
GLOBAL STYLE LOCK — copy these constraints into every design and coding task:
Premium stylized cyberpunk 3D, futuristic high-rise residences, dark titanium, premium techwear, matte
charcoal, navy, layered glass, restrained cyan/violet/magenta emissive accents, believable furniture scale,
cinematic warm/cool lighting. UI remains a coherent high-end science-fiction game HUD — including finance,
tasks, building editor, character creator, inventory, shops, AI and onboarding.
Forbidden style drift: generic SaaS dashboards; flat unstyled forms; realistic modern-day casual UI;
Roblox/blocky character art; 2D pixel art; cartoon/anime mismatch; medieval fantasy costume; excessive
rainbow glow; random asset palettes; plain default browser controls; different graphic styles per module.
Safe customization: allow controlled cyberpunk color variants, hairstyles, faces, wearable items and interiors
WITHOUT leaving the shared cyberpunk art universe. Finance can be calmer and more readable, but must
still match the same tokens, typography, framing and navigation.

ART CONSISTENCY CONTRACT
Scene / architecture
Stylized physically coherent 3D; modular floors
and wall openings; reusable materials; purposeful
signage; believable proportions; constrained
isometric camera; cozy warm interiors against
blue/purple city lighting.
Character / animation
One consistent body rig and asset scale; clothes
fit the same character; same face, hair and outfit
visible in character creator, home, equipment,
wardrobe, boss and relevant overlays. No
silhouette replacements between screens.
Game UI
Shared components for navigation, inventory
slots, action buttons, iconography, tooltip,
typography, focus outlines, rewards and alerts.
Neon must highlight meaning, not dominate
reading.
Store / economy
Cosmetic style stays cyberpunk; no medieval
fantasy jackets or unrelated assets unless
redesigned into the world. All shop previews must
match the equipped 3D result.
VISUAL ACCEPTANCE CONTRACT
Mandatory visual quality gates
01. Screenshot every affected screen at desktop
and mobile viewports.
02. Compare palette, 3D proportions, material
scale, typography, HUD density and scene
composition to the references.
03. Verify actual 3D and interactive controls, not a
static screenshot.
04. Reject obvious placeholders as final assets.
05. Inspect face/body clipping, outfit
intersections, hair/headwear collisions, wall
collision and camera behavior.
06. Test reduced-motion and low graphics modes.
07. Track deviations and fix before claiming visual
acceptance.
Locked style tokens
Colors: #080D18, #10192C, #32E6F4, #9067FF,
#EE5ACB, #FFBC62, #EDF4FF.
Any added semantic colors must harmonize with
these and remain accessible.
Explicit instruction
Do not silently reinterpret “Cyberpunk” as
neon-only styling. The architectural world, avatar,
garments, item silhouettes and UI need to share
one art direction.
ONE 3D UNIVERSE
ONE DESIGN SYSTEM
NO STYLE DRIFT

3D CUSTOMIZER PROMPT  /  SELECTABLE TEXT
CHARACTER CREATOR MASTER PROMPT (EN): Build a truly editable 3D character creator, not a static
picture. The player controls hair style/length/color; face preset and jaw; eye shape, iris color and eyebrow
shape; nose bridge/tip shape; ear shape/size and earrings; lips/mouth; skin tone; optional facial hair,
makeup and compatible body presets. The same customizable character must render across all game
scenes. Design a cyberpunk avatar preview with switchable head close-up / full-body camera, orbit, zoom,
reset, comparison and live updates.
Minimum editable MVP content: 12 hairstyles, 12 hair colors, 6 eye shapes, 8 iris colors, 6 eyebrows, 6 nose
shapes, 4 ear shapes, 6 mouth/lip types, 6 face/jaw presets, 12 skin tones, 3 body presets, plus optional
beard, tattoos, piercings and cyberware where supported by the model pipeline.
Behavior: Changing an appearance control must visibly update the 3D mesh, morph target, attached
component or material. Do not implement fake sliders that only change numbers or captions. Provide
curated presets where high-quality morph targets are unavailable.

3D asset contract
Create a single avatar rig compatible with game
locomotion and idle/action animations. Use
reusable modular hairstyle meshes with correct
scalp placement, eye/skin material variants and
facial morph targets where feasible. Anchor
eyewear, hats and earrings at consistent
attachment points. Meshes need correct pivots
and scale.
Face editor
Tabs: Hair, Face, Eyes, Brows, Nose, Ears, Mouth,
Skin, Body, Makeup, Accessories and Outfit. Click
a facial tab → camera smoothly frames the head;
choose a preset or validated slider → immediate
3D update; switch back to full body for clothing.
Allow Save, Cancel, Reset and Randomize.
Character persistence
Save a structured appearance configuration
server-side, including avatar version/model
references, selected morph values or preset IDs
and equipped cosmetic item instance IDs. Load it
consistently after refresh and on mobile.
INITIAL FREE CLOTHES + RULES
FREE STARTER WARDROBE
On first character creation, grant a starter
cosmetic collection ONCE, server-side. Offer at
least 3 complete free cyberpunk outfits (Street
Runner, Neo-Corporate and Night Technician) with
mix-and-match pieces. Suggested free options: 6
tops, 4 bottoms, 4 shoe styles, 3 outerwear pieces
and 4 small accessories, compatible with avatar
variants.
Starter selection
Preview complete looks and individual layers.
Choose a full starter set or create a custom
combination from owned free items. Show the
exact final 3D look before confirmation. All chosen
items must remain owned and can be re-equipped
later.
Important boundary
Appearance cosmetics are independent from
combat equipment bonuses unless an item
explicitly declares both capabilities; do not let
wardrobe garments inadvertently alter boss
damage. No real-money requirement or external
payment for starter customization.
