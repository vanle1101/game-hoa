CHARACTER CREATOR MASTER PROMPT (EN): Build a truly editable 3D character creator, not a static
picture. The player controls hair style/length/color; face preset and jaw; eye shape, iris color and eyebrow
shape; nose bridge/tip shape; ear shape/size and earrings; lips/mouth; skin tone; optional facial hair,
makeup and compatible body presets. The same customizable character must render across all game
scenes. Design a cyberpunk avatar preview with switchable head close-up / full-body camera, orbit, zoom,
reset, comparison and live updates.
Minimum editable MVP content: 12 hairstyles, 12 hair colors, 6 eye shapes, 8 iris colors, 6 eyebrows, 6 nose
shapes, 4 ear shapes, 6 mouth/lip types, 6 face/jaw presets, 12 skin tones, 3 body presets, plus optional
beard, tattoos, piercings and cyberware where supported by the model pipeline.
Behavior: Changing an appearance control must visibly update the 3D mesh, morph target, attached
component or material. Do not implement fake sliders that only change numbers or captions. Provide
curated presets where high-quality morph targets are unavailable.
