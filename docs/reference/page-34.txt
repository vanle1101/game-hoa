SUPPLEMENT 06/07
SERVER & AVATAR CONTRACT
Ownership and unlocks
Use a catalog of cosmetic definitions + per-user
owned item instances/entitlements. Make starter
item grants idempotent. Award optional cosmetic
items from campaigns, bosses, achievements and
game Gold shops. No real-money transactions.
Store appearance history only if useful, and never
grant purchases merely from UI state.
Wearable compatibility
Slots: Hair, Headwear, Face/Eyes, Earrings, Top,
Outerwear, Bottoms, Shoes, Gloves, Back,
Cyberware and optional Full Outfit. Define mesh
compatibility and priority for clipping prevention.
Full outfits may lock some slots; disclose this
before equipping.
Persistence
Transactions: wallet ledger + cosmetic ownership
+ event log. Each equipped item must be owned,
available and compatible. Protect
purchase/equip/undo from two simultaneous tabs
and network retries.
ACCEPTANCE TESTS
Must-pass QA cases
01 Create avatar, customize eye/nose/ear/hair
independently; visibly confirm changes.
02 Pick a free starter outfit; no Gold is deducted.
03 Reload and verify every face setting and outfit
layer persist.
04 Buy one jacket with game Gold; exact debit,
one grant.
05 Try duplicate purchase/retry and concurrent
tabs; no duplicate debit or grants.
06 Equip new jacket; verify in wardrobe, 3D home
and character view.
07 Remove jacket/re-equip original outfit; original
owned inventory remains.
08 Insufficient Gold or locked item does not
unlock.
09 Changing clothing does not alter finance
balance or combat stats accidentally.
10 Verify headwear/hair, face masks, shoes, arm
joints and animations do not clip badly.
11 Verify desktop, mobile, low-graphics and
reloaded sessions.
