Design a fully featured 3D house construction mode interface for LIFE//LEVEL, a premium cyberpunk life
simulation RPG. Inspired by the interaction depth of sophisticated house-building simulation games but
with an entirely original visual identity. Large central 3D orthographic isometric construction viewport,
partially built futuristic apartment, clearly visible square floor grid, individually editable wall segments, a
rectangular room being drawn with a translucent preview, structural placement indicators. Bottom
construction toolbar with walls, rooms, floors, doors, windows, stairs, roof, materials and demolition tool
icons. Right contextual catalog, top currency and building cost display, undo/redo, confirm and cancel.
Premium dark futuristic game UI, restrained cyan and violet highlights. Clean 16:9 desktop
screenshot-style concept, readable spatial composition.
